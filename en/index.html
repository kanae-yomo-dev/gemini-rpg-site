Gemini in Flux

The other half of your soul — never let go.

Emotion becomes power.
In the Afterworld, walk beside a same-sex partner in this turn-based dark fantasy RPG,
where your words and choices shape the bond between you—and which of four endings you reach.

View on Steam

Coming Summer 2027 ／ Windows & macOS ／ Japanese & English

STORY

You awaken in a quiet land shaped by the memories of the dead.
Your own past is gone—but beside you stands a partner, who gives you your name.

Gather the scattered shards of the shattered Mirror of Samsara, they say, and you may reclaim your lost past—and a place to return to.
A talking black cat, a demon of flame, a spirit of wind—together with companions met along the road, the two of you set out in search of the shards.

Choose your protagonist: Mizuki or Nagi. Each walks a different story, with a different partner.

FEATURES

Emotion System

Joy, Anger, Sadness, Fear. Emotions stirred by the events of the journey are reflected directly in battle stats and character expressions.

Strategic Turn-Based Battles

Strike elemental and creature-type weaknesses, and swap weapons to match the foe in classic command combat. The Sharp Eye blessing gradually reveals what your enemies are hiding.

Choices Shape the Ending

Your words slowly change the bond between you and your partner. Four endings await each protagonist.

At the bottom of still waters, the story begins.
