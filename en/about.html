ABOUT

Gemini in Flux is an indie RPG developed by kanae (studio: Salmon Dance). The story, the pixel art, and the Unity code are all built side by side with AI, like two runners in a three-legged race. If either of us were missing, the human or the AI, this game would not exist.

WHY THIS GAME

The reason is simple: the game I want to play does not exist yet.

  • I want a story-rich RPG where a same-sex couple takes center stage, with their love portrayed head-on!
  • I want classic turn-based battles that are strategic without being overcomplicated!
  • I want exploration full of hidden rooms and secret stairways, the kind that lights up your brain!

Plenty of great games deliver one of these. I could not find one that delivers all three. So I am building it myself. That is how Gemini in Flux began. Aiming for a Summer 2027 release on Steam.
